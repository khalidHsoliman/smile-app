# Demo case photos

Drop two photographs here and a **Load the demo case** button appears on the
upload step. Without them the button stays hidden and nothing else changes.

    smile.jpg      frontal, natural smile, relaxed lips
    retracted.jpg  the same patient, cheek retractors in place

`.jpeg` and `.png` work too.

## Before you add them

These files ship with the application, so whoever deploys it is publishing
them. Two separate permissions are needed and only one of them is about
copyright:

- **The photographer's**, which a licence or a work-for-hire agreement covers.
- **The patient's**, which it does not. An identifiable face used in a dental
  demo presents that person as a patient; in the EU and UK that is health data
  under GDPR Article 9, and most jurisdictions also recognise a personality or
  publicity right independent of copyright.

A Creative Commons or stock licence grants the first and is silent on the
second. Use photographs taken with a signed model release that covers
marketing and demonstration use.

## Choosing the pair

The design is judged on these, so the pair does the demo as much as the
software does:

- **Frontal and level.** Head roughly upright, both pupils visible; the whole
  design is levelled against the interpupillary line.
- **Additive cases convince.** Worn, short or spaced teeth, where the design
  covers what is there. A design narrower or shorter than the existing teeth
  leaves the originals visible around it, which reads as a bug rather than a
  proposal.
- **Same session, same lighting.** The alignment corrects for distance and a
  little head pitch, not for a different room a month later.
- **Retractors clear of the anterior teeth.** The alignment locks onto the
  front teeth and their biting edge; a retractor across them is the one thing
  that reliably defeats it.
